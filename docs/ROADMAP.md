# 🧭 Outscape Project Roadmap

This document outlines the milestones and future plans for **Outscape**, developed as an open-source outdoor companion powered by open-weight AI.

---

## 📍 Milestone 1: Hacktoberfest 2026 (Week 1 — "Touch Grass")
*Target: October 5–11, 2026*

- [x] **Project Foundation:** Project architecture, design system, and open-source licensing.
- [ ] **Core Open-Weight AI Engine:**
  - Context-aware nature prompt generation via **Google Gemma 2** (micro-itineraries, biomes, seasonal highlights).
  - Robust offline-first fallback for disconnected hiking trails.
- [ ] **Hands-Free Audio Experience ("Pocket Mode"):**
  - Text-to-Speech (TTS) integration with natural cadence and tone.
  - Background audio playback capability (phone locked or kept in pocket).
  - Subtle sound cues signaling upcoming outdoor points of interest.
- [ ] **Mobile-First Outdoor UI:**
  - High-contrast, sunlight-readable natural palette.
  - Minimalist controls designed for minimal screen engagement.
- [ ] **Trail & Step Simulator:**
  - Real-time GPS tracker with step simulator for indoor testing and demo verification.
- [ ] **Deployment & Submission:**
  - Cloud deployment (Render / DigitalOcean) and official DEV.to submission article.

---

## 📍 Milestone 2: Biome Expansion & Rich Nature Knowledge
*Target: Post-Hacktoberfest Q4 2026*

- [ ] **Crowdsourced Local Nature Packs:** Open JSON/GeoJSON schemas for local parks, bird migration paths, and flora species.
- [ ] **Multimodal Visual Verification (Optional):** Lightweight on-device image classification (MobileNet / Gemma Multimodal) to snap a picture of a leaf or bark if desired.
- [ ] **Multi-Language Voice Support:** Native narration support in English, Japanese, Portuguese, and Spanish.

---

## 📍 Milestone 3: Hardware & Wearables
*Target: Q1 2027*

- [ ] **Smartwatch & Bluetooth Remote Integration:** Control next/pause cues directly from wearable buttons.
- [ ] **Arduino / Edge Sensor Companion:** Exploring integration with environmental sensors (e.g., Arduino Uno Q) for temperature, altitude, and humidity sensing on the trail.
